# Research

**Managed Team:** Capture Team

Build evidence-backed opportunity intelligence.

## Assign work

> Research [opportunity] for [organization]: customer needs, requirements, incumbent and competitors if known, acquisition context, risks, and unknowns. Cite sources.

Use real authorized organizational inputs or clearly labeled fictional data for practice. Never assume connected systems or permission to send anything.

## Review work

**Expected deliverable:** Opportunity research brief with sources and gaps.

**Human approval gate:** Validate the evidence and authorize capture planning.

## Customize

Research sources, depth, brief format.
