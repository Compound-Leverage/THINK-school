# Capture

**Managed Team:** Capture Team

Plan the pursuit and decide next actions.

## Assign work

> Using approved research on [opportunity], prepare a capture brief with positioning, capability gaps, engagement actions, owners, milestones, and a recommended Go/No-Go. Mark assumptions.

Use real authorized organizational inputs or clearly labeled fictional data for practice. Never assume connected systems or permission to send anything.

## Review work

**Expected deliverable:** Capture plan, action list, and decision recommendation.

**Human approval gate:** Make the pursuit decision and approve engagement.

## Customize

Owners, capture strategy, decision gates.
