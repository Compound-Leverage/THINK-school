# Build and Operate AI-Native Services

AI-native services sell **finished work**, not access to agents. The operating approach is a human strategist directing an AI-assisted team with explicit scope, approval, and quality controls.

## Two ways to apply the model

**Inside an organization:** Support a department or growth team by taking recurring work assignments through an accountable operator.

**As a service business:** Package a repeatable outcome for external customers, starting with a narrow job before adding ongoing assignments.

## Service design

1. **Choose a buyer and recurring workload:** Identify work with clear demand, measurable quality, and business value.
2. **Define the deliverable:** Specify the customer-ready artifact and what completion means.
3. **Set boundaries:** Clarify inclusions, exclusions, turnarounds, required customer inputs, and escalation triggers.
4. **Assign the operator:** Make one human accountable for intake, orchestration, review, and delivery.
5. **Select the team:** Use educational plugins to practice roles and workflows. Production customer delivery requires separate access, privacy, authorization, and operational controls.
6. **Price the service:** Consider fixed-scope projects, recurring subscriptions, or agreed task orders. Price for the value and delivery responsibility, not just token usage.
7. **Create a repeatable loop:** Intake → Plan → Execute → Review → Approve → Deliver → Improve.

## Example service: Proposal support

**Customer request:** Develop a response to a qualified opportunity.

**Deliverables:** Requirements matrix, response outline, draft proposal, compliance review, and submission-ready package following human approval.

**Required inputs:** Solicitation, customer capabilities, past performance, pricing authority, submission rules, and deadline.

**Quality controls:** Evidence checks, compliance review, authorized pricing, and final customer sign-off.

**Commercial follow-through:** Begin with a clearly defined piece of work; expand to recurring proposal or capture support when there is sustained demand.

## Protect the customer and your business

Never represent an educational plugin as a secured multi-tenant production service. Establish customer-specific confidentiality, access controls, authorized integrations, contractual terms, data handling, quality review, and auditability before handling live engagements.
