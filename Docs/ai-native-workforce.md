# Working AI-Native: The THINK Workforce Model

THINK School teaches how to organize, direct, and evaluate work with AI-native teams. Educational plugins are portable learning tools; they are not Compound Leverage's proprietary customer delivery runtime.

## The operating model

**Subject Matter Expert / Customer → THINK Strategist → AI Team → Reviewed deliverable**

- **Subject Matter Expert / Customer:** Defines objectives, constraints, context, and acceptance criteria; approves consequential decisions.
- **THINK Strategist:** Turns requests into scoped jobs; selects specialists; supplies input context; manages dependencies, exceptions, and quality.
- **Team orchestration:** Coordinates the order of specialist work and clear handoffs.
- **Specialist Digital Employees:** Produce bounded research, analysis, draft artifacts, or reviews.
- **Human approval gates:** Validate readiness, claims, business decisions, and external submission.

## From assignment to finished work

1. **Intake:** Record request, desired output, due date, constraints, and source material.
2. **Plan:** Define job scope, required roles, inputs, dependencies, milestones, and acceptance criteria.
3. **Execute:** Assign bounded tasks; document assumptions and evidence; hand off outputs.
4. **Review:** Check factual accuracy, completeness, compliance, tone, provenance, and confidentiality.
5. **Approve:** Obtain explicit human sign-off at consequential gates.
6. **Deliver:** Provide the finished work and record decisions, unresolved risks, and next steps.

### Example: Proposal assessment

**Assignment:** Evaluate a fictional grant opportunity for a fictional organization.

**Research specialist:** Creates discovery brief, assumptions, sources, and preliminary Go/No-Go recommendation.

**Approval gate:** Human reviews and approves the decision before detailed analysis.

**Analyst:** Evaluates fit, risks, effort, and financial assumptions. Never invents bid-sizing rules.

**Strategy and drafting specialists:** Build the narrative and proposed response only after authorization.

**Reviewer:** Checks the finished package against the original requirements.

## Practice checklist

- Can another person understand the assignment without reading the entire conversation?
- Are outputs, sources, and assumptions explicit?
- Are job dependencies and human gates visible?
- Does quality review test the deliverable rather than merely its creation process?
- Are real customer data and secrets excluded from public or sample workflows?
