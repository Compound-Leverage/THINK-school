# Sample Proposal Discovery Brief — fictional training example

> **Illustrative only.** All organizations, procurement details, and assumptions are fictional. No solicitation has been reviewed and no live proposal assessment was executed.

**Bidder:** Harborlight Systems (fictional)  
**Opportunity:** Digital Service Improvement Initiative (fictional)  
**Role:** Chase — preliminary research and Go/No-Go gate

## Discovery summary

Harborlight's assumed analytics and IT modernization capabilities could be relevant to the fictional initiative. Because no authentic solicitation or verified evaluation criteria exist, the opportunity cannot be treated as bid-ready.

| Review area | Preliminary finding | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Mission fit | Potential alignment with digital service modernization | Assumed |
| Mandatory requirements | Not provided | Unknown |
| Evaluation criteria | Not provided | Unknown |
| Past performance | Not provided | Unknown |
| Pricing ceiling | Not provided | Unknown |
| Submission deadline | Not provided | Unknown |

## Recommendation

**Conditional Go for further discovery only; not approval to bid.** Obtain the actual solicitation, bidder profile, mandatory requirements, and evaluation factors before Priya performs detailed fit, financial feasibility, or pricing analysis.

**Human gate:** Approve progression to detailed assessment only after confirming required inputs. Otherwise request more research or stop.
