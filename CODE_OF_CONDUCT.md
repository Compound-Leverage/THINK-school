# Code of Conduct

We welcome constructive, professional collaboration in THINK School issues, pull requests, and discussions.

Be respectful, assume good intent, and discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, disclosure of private information, and spam are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content, restrict participation, or close conversations to keep the project productive. Report conduct concerns privately to partners@compoundleverage.com. Reports will be reviewed as appropriate; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in all circumstances.
