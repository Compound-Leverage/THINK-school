# Security Policy

## Report vulnerabilities privately

Do not open public issues containing exploitable vulnerabilities, credentials, customer information, or sensitive logs. Report concerns to **partners@compoundleverage.com** with affected files, reproduction steps, impact, and a safe way to contact you.

We will review reports and determine appropriate remediation and disclosure. No response or remediation timeline is guaranteed.

## Scope

This repository distributes educational plugin instructions and manifests. Third-party AI platforms and connectors maintain their own security programs. Users must review permissions, authorize connected accounts, and approve external actions.
